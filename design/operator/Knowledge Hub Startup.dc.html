We hold the shares
You bought uptime. After a power cut we unseal it for you, remotely or on site. Nobody at your desk needs a runbook.
You hold the shares
We keep zero copies. Your security officer performs the test unseal today and owns every restart after that.
Said plainly, because you should not learn it during an outage
These shares protect the data if the box is stolen or its drive is thrown away. They do not protect you from whoever operates the box while it is running. And if all five are lost there is no recovery — the vault is re-initialised and every source credential is entered again.
